A little about me

Curious by nature.
A builder by choice.

I enjoy exploring how things work, connecting ideas, and turning curiosity into practical projects. My interests live at the intersection of software, creativity, systems thinking, and human understanding.

I'm especially interested in technology that helps people retain context, understand complexity, and remain in control of the systems they use. To me, building well means caring about not just what a system can do, but why it exists and whom it serves.

I'm learning by making things, documenting what I discover, and sharing work openly so others can explore, adapt, and build on it.

01. Curiosity before certainty

Ask questions, investigate carefully, and leave room for new evidence to change the picture.

02. People before technology

Tools should support human understanding and agency, not quietly replace them.

03. Build with care

Favor clear foundations, honest documentation, responsible choices, and steady improvement.
