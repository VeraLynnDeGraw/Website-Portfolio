About the builder

Curiosity into
something useful.

I'm Vera Lynn DeGraw, an independent builder exploring the intersection of software development, artificial intelligence, and human creativity.

I'm fascinated by complex systems, creative coding, and the ways people and AI can collaborate to turn ideas into useful tools. I learn by building, experimenting, documenting, and improving real projects.

I'm also interested in the principles behind good technology: preserving context, designing responsibly, making systems understandable, and supporting open collaboration.

This portfolio is a living record of my work, my experiments, and what I'm learning along the way.

I'm interested in technology not only for what it can do, but for how thoughtfully we choose to build it.

Principles behind the work

  • Understand before changing Learn how a system works before trying to improve it.
  • Preserve context and intent Understand why something exists, not just how it works.
  • Build responsibly Value transparency, human judgment, and thoughtful design.
  • Learn in the open Document progress, share useful work, and improve through iteration.
